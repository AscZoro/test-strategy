# Claims Adjudication

## TC006 Deductible Not Met Flow

- **Work Item ID:** 1572
- **Description:** Verify claim auto-pends to Deductible Review queue when deductible is not met
- **Precondition:** Member is enrolled. OON deductible balance is $1000.
- **Test Step:** 1
- **Test Step Description:** Submit out-of-network outpatient diagnostic claim for processing.
- **Test Step Expected Result:** Claim is blocked from direct adjudication, status transitions to 'Auto-Pend', and the record routes to 'Deductible Review' queue.

## TC009 Edge - Exact Deductible Balance

- **Work Item ID:** 1572
- **Description:** Verify allocation logic when claim amount exactly matches outstanding deductible
- **Precondition:** Member OON deductible balance is exactly $150.
- **Test Step:** 1
- **Test Step Description:** Submit claim matching deductible balance exactly.
- **Test Step Expected Result:** System applies full $150 to deductible; plan covers $0, member responsibility is $150, new deductible balance updates to $0.

## TC012 Negative - Missing Diagnostic Code

- **Work Item ID:** 1572
- **Description:** Verify validation layer flags claims missing mandatory diagnostic fields
- **Precondition:** Member is active.
- **Test Step:** 1
- **Test Step Description:** Attempt to ingest claim without diagnosis descriptors.
- **Test Step Expected Result:** Ingestion drops out with validation error alert mapping code 'ERR-VAL-99' (Missing Mandatory Diagnosis Data).

## TC015 Deductible Partial Match

- **Work Item ID:** 1572
- **Description:** Verify partial deductible application when claim amount exceeds remaining deductible balance
- **Precondition:** Member OON deductible balance is $200.
- **Test Step:** 1
- **Test Step Description:** Process out-of-network claim through the adjudication engine.
- **Test Step Expected Result:** System applies $200 to satisfy remaining deductible, then subjects the remaining $300 to standard 30% coinsurance ($90 member responsibility).
